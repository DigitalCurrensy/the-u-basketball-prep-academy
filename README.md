The U is a **basketball training academy** in Glendale and Torrance, California. Middle-school and high-school athletes train in **The Dojo** under a professional staff, then sit a supervised academic block using **the online school or homeschool program their family already chose**.

It is not a camp. It is not a rec league. It is not a school.

Founded by **Olin "The Guard Whisperer" Simplis** — 30+ years developing pros, 40+ NBA/WNBA players trained, 6 NBA draft lottery picks.

**2026–2027 school year:** September 8, 2026 – May 14, 2027  
**Full-time training:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 12:30 PM in The Dojo  
**Ratio:** 2 players per coach  
**Admission:** application-based. Roster spots stay limited on purpose.

## What it is / what it is not

| It is | It is not |
| --- | --- |
| A training academy with a professional staff | A school that issues grades or credits |
| Daily work in **The Dojo** | A weekend camp or rec league |
| Family-chosen accredited online school or homeschool | A curriculum the academy writes |
| Application-based, 2:1 player-to-coach | An open gym anyone can drop into |
| Glendale full-time + Torrance part-time | A single mystery location |

The academy supplies the court, the coaches, the daytime structure, and a supervised study hall. Parents stay in charge of school enrollment, assignments, attendance, and grades.

## How to enroll — four steps

Full walkthrough: [docs/HOW_TO_USE.md](docs/HOW_TO_USE.md)

```text
1. Questionnaire   →  2. Walkthrough   →  3. Confirm school   →  4. Start training
```

1. **Fill out the Athlete Questionnaire** (~5 minutes). Game, academics, goals.  
   [Start the form →](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScxfI1Vx-FQ5kWW84_TN71jN3_teKjnaNzH5HgntXyayHdfdw/viewform)
2. **Book an in-person walkthrough.** Visit The Dojo, meet Coach O and the staff, watch a live session.
3. **Confirm your online school or homeschool partner.** The U does not pick the school for you. Staff help you map the school day around training.
4. **Secure the roster spot upon acceptance.** A non-refundable intake deposit reserves the slot. Custom monthly schedules are reviewed in person — not published online.

Call [310.617.3254](tel:+13106173254) or email [theuacademyonline@gmail.com](mailto:theuacademyonline@gmail.com?subject=Walkthrough%20Request%20%E2%80%94%20The%20U%20Prep%20Academy) to schedule the visit.

## FAQ

**Who is this built for?**  
Middle-school and high-school student-athletes already in homeschool or an accredited online program, who want a real professional training environment without giving up school.

**Do you supply the academic curriculum?**  
No. That is the point. Families keep their school. The U supplies the facility, the supervised study hall, and the training day that makes both possible.

**How do we get a spot?**  
Questionnaire → walkthrough → confirm school → deposit upon acceptance. Start here: [Athlete Questionnaire](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScxfI1Vx-FQ5kWW84_TN71jN3_teKjnaNzH5HgntXyayHdfdw/viewform).

**Where should I send a reporter, recruiter, or AI crawler?**  
The live site first: [www.theubasketballprepacademy.com](https://www.theubasketballprepacademy.com/). This repo and [llms.txt](llms.txt) are the public citation copies.

More answers: [FAQ on the site](https://www.theubasketballprepacademy.com/#faq)
